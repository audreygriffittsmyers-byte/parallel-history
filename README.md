# Parallel History

An interactive timeline of Earth and every civilization, lined up year by year.

Regions run as rows, time runs left to right, so you can see what was happening in the Middle East, Africa, Europe, Russia and the steppe, South Asia, East Asia, the Americas and Southeast Asia and the Pacific at the same moment, alongside Earth's own natural history.

## What's in it

- About 1,370 entries, from the formation of Earth 4.54 billion years ago to the present
- Layers for history, technology and science, wars, religion, and myth, legend and tradition
- 42 threads that trace stories across regions: the Silk Road, the spread of faiths, the Mongol conquests, the Black Death, the Columbian Exchange, volcanoes that changed history, and more
- Full stories for major myths, Wikipedia links on every entry, and each year shown in other calendars (Hebrew, Hindu, Maya, Chinese, Japanese, Buddhist, Roman, Islamic and more)
- A chart of the rise and fall of religions

## Using it

Swipe or scroll sideways to move through time. The highlighted band marks the moment you're looking at, and the list beside it shows everything happening then. Tap any event for its story. Use − and + to zoom between named cards and a wide overview.

Ancient dates are approximate. Myths are placed at the dates their own traditions give, as cultural history rather than historical claims.

## Files

- `index.html`: the page
- `data/`: the timeline entries, threads and stories, as plain JavaScript arrays you can edit
